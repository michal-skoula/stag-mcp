- [ ] `ucitel` namespace: **mapped, not yet built.** 13 endpoints, 4 of them `AD`-only writes/reads of personal data (`*UcitelOsobniUdaje`, whose type carries `rodCislo`, `datumNarozeni`, `pohlavi` — off limits). `ng_ucitel` is an empty WADL stub. `selectUciteleTerminStatistikaPoctuStudentu` is IP-allowlisted and 500s for us even authenticated. `getGarant_ProgramOborByRC` is deliberately excluded: called with no params it anonymously dumps 1060 rows with 329 rodná čísla in the clear, and `getGarant_ProgramOborByUcitIdno` answers the same question from a STAG id.

  That leaves five worth wrapping, in two tools. **`search-ucitele`**: `getUciteleKatedry` is the backbone, not `najdiUcitelePodleJmena`. The name search looks like the better filter — case- and diacritics-insensitive, SQL wildcards, no row cap (`%a%` = 7764), 500s unless it holds one literal character — but it returns **only `platnost=A` rows**, and `platnost` does not mean "currently teaching". Student demonstrators and doctoral assistants run labs with `platnost: N` and `zamestnanec: A` (Bc. Denisa Šperlová, ucitIdno 301402, KIV, first-year labs in 2026/2027). Twelve of KIV's 283 `platnost=N` people were checked and not one appears in a search on their own surname; every apparent hit is a homonym with a different `ucitIdno`. `getUcitelInfo?ucitIdno=` returns them fine, so they exist and are merely unsearchable. Building the tool on the name search means answering "no such person" for exactly the people a first-year asks about, which is the worst failure available here. So fetch `getUciteleKatedry` with `jenAktualni` present and match client-side, the same fetch-all-and-filter-locally shape `SearchPredmetyTool` already uses for `getPredmetyByFakulta` (KIV: 319 rows without the flag, 602 with, the extra 283 being the `platnost=N` set). That endpoint needs a department, so a university-wide name search means fanning out across departments or running both endpoints and merging on `ucitIdno`; keep `najdiUcitelePodleJmena` as the fast path and treat an empty result from it as incomplete rather than as an answer. Its `jenAktualni`/`zobrazitExternisty` are presence-only booleans whose value STAG ignores and whose names read inverted, so expose them as explicit `include_former` / `employees_only` flags rather than passing them through. Rows from either endpoint are the bare 7-field shape (the department listing adds `email`), so contact details still need a fan-out to `getUcitelInfo`, or paginate and enrich only the page returned. **`get-ucitel-info`**: `getUcitelInfo` by `ucitIdno`, joined with `vizitka/getVizitkaByUcitidno` for room and phone line (`getUcitelInfo`'s own `zkrBudovy`/`cisloMistnosti` are sparse — KIV 39/316, KKY 0/128 — and agree with the vizitka when present), plus `getGarant_ProgramOborByUcitIdno` for what they guarantee. A student teacher has no business card at all (`getVizitkaByUcitidno` returns `[]`), so the department listing's `email` is the only contact STAG holds for them and the tool must degrade to it rather than look broken. Same `telefon`-is-free-text and office-hours-are-unmaintained rules as `StudyAdvisorTool`; reuse that reasoning rather than re-litigating it.

  `getUcitelePredmetu` and `getUciteleRoakce` are the pieces that close the loop from a timetable or a subject back to a person, which is what `teacher_id` is surfaced for everywhere else. `getUcitelePredmetu` is mostly redundant with `predmety/getPredmetInfo` (which already has `garantiUcitIdno`, `prednasejiciUcitIdno`, `cviciciUcitIdno`), and its `pr` rows encode the role as a positional `AAAN`/`NNAN` flag string in a field called `typAkce`, which means something entirely different on its `ra` rows. `getUciteleRoakce` is the cleaner of the two and the only way to resolve one timetable event to `ucitIdno`, since `getRozvrhByStudent` gives teacher names as text only. Fold whichever is needed into the existing subject and timetable tools instead of giving either its own.

  Auth is the trap here: the docs mark every read endpoint "can be called without login" and three of them 401 anyway (`najdiUcitelePodleJmena`, `getUcitelInfo`, `getUciteleKatedry`), so both tools want `RequiresStagLogin`. Full findings in `stag-quirks`.

- [ ] `vsp` is vizualizace studijniho planu. **Michal is building this one himself.** Output shape is now mapped, notes below and in the stag-quirks skill.
  Two endpoints, and the join between them is the whole point: neither half is worth much alone. `getStudijniPlan?stplIdno=` is anonymous, 276 KB for one bachelor plan, and is the only place the subjects satisfying a blok are listed (`programy/getBlokInfo` gives the credit rule and nothing else): plan → `skupinySegmentu` → `segmenty` → `bloky` → `predmety`, each with `nazev{valueCs,valueEn}`, `kredity`, `rozsah`, `typZk`, `doporucenyRocnik`, `doporucenySemestr`, `vyznamPredmetu`. `getStudent?osCislo=` is the progress side: `rokyStudia` (one per year, each with its own `stplIdno`) → `obory` → `predmety`, per subject `statut`, `stavAbsolvovani`, `znamkaHodnoceni`, `znamkaPokus`, `uspesneAbsolvovany`, `uznano`, `praveStudovan`, `zapsanProPristiRok`.
  Verified the join produces a real degree audit: all 23 of a test student's enrolled and passed subjects matched a block in the current plan, zero unmatched, yielding per-block "need 132 credits over 27 subjects, have 31 over 7, 45 in progress, these 11 still required".
  Three things to handle before writing it. `getStudent` is unusable as JSON — `StudentVRoce["student"]` cycles back to `Student["rokyStudia"]`, the 104 KB body is truncated mid-structure with the cycle trace appended, and it parses at no `json_decode` depth; `Accept: application/xml` returns a clean 31 KB, so `StagClient` needs an XML path first. Its `osCislo` is mandatory but ignored, so resolve identity from the ticket and never echo the requested number back. And the plan payload must be reduced server-side, never handed to the model whole — the EU Track blok alone is 37 statut-C subjects that should collapse to a count.
  Smaller traps: `pocetSemestru` is 0 on every plan (take it from `programy/getPlanInfo`), `poznamkaVerejna` is a literal placeholder, subject names exist only on the plan side, and the same subject can appear twice in one blok (`UTS/TV`, two semesters at 1 credit against a 2-credit minimum) or once in two bloky (`KIV/PRO`), so neither dedupe nor blind summing is safe. `podminujiciPredmety` is always empty because ZČU abolished prerequisites, not because the field is broken — keep the mapping.

- [x] **StudyAdvisorTool:** shipped as `get-study-advisor`, and `study_advisor` is out of `who-am-i`. Merges the `studReferentka*` block on `student/getStudentInfo` with `vizitka/getVizitkaByUcitidno` for the office building and room (the only place STAG records it — `getUcitelInfo`'s `zkrBudovy`/`cisloMistnosti` are always null) and published office hours. Optional `os_cislo`, resolved from the ticket otherwise. A refused or missing business card degrades to empty offices rather than failing, so name/email/phone still answer.
  `phone` is passed through verbatim and deliberately not normalised: STAG's `telefon` is free text holding 4-digit extensions, full numbers, mobiles, foreign numbers and even room codes (`UN325`), so prefixing would invent wrong numbers. Referentky do hold proper full numbers. Phone extensions, `preferovanaLinka`, `www` and the STAG login were all dropped as noise. Office hours are deliberately **not** read from STAG, even though two endpoints serve them (`uredniHodiny` on the business card, and `vizitka/getUredniHodinyPracoviste` for a whole workplace — both tested, both work). Nobody maintains that copy: study offices are required to keep úřední hodiny and publish them on the faculty website, which is where everyone actually reads them, and neither test referentka had anything in STAG. A stale record is worse than none, since an agent reports a plausible Tuesday 13:00 as fact and the student finds a locked door. Instead `office_hours_note` is always set, telling the agent to look on the faculty site and naming it, derived from the advisor's email domain rather than a lookup table. Reasoning is in a comment on `officeHoursNote()`; revisit if STAG's copy ever becomes the maintained one. `vizitka/telefonniSeznamZCU` (the real phone directory) 401s for a student role, so there is no bulk staff-contact route; `users/getOsoba` 403s and `cro_ucitele` 500s.

- [x] **GetZnamkyTool:** finished, as one tool merging three endpoints. `znamky/getZnamkyByStudent` (grades, points, attempts, dates, examiners) + `student/getStudentPredmetyAbsolvoval` (subject names and credits, which znamky does not return at all) + `znamky/typyHodnoceni` (the grading scales). Defaults to the current semester so it answers "what's done, what's left" with no arguments; `all_years` widens to the whole degree, plus rok/semestr/katedra/zkratka filters. No pagination — a whole degree tops out around a couple hundred rows, well inside a token-safe response, so the full matching set always comes back in one call, newest period first. `semestr` accepts `""` as an alias for `"%"` so an agent that leaves it blank still gets both; a `rok`/`semestr` outside its small validated set is rejected before calling STAG, since upstream a garbage value silently returns nothing rather than erroring, which would otherwise read as "you have no grades". Filtering is client-side because `pracZkr`/`zkrPredm` are exact-match and case-sensitive upstream.

  Business logic lives in `app/Services/Znamky` (`StudyRecordService` fetches, `StudyRecord` summarises, `Subject` maps one enrolment, `GradeScales` wraps the codebook) rather than in the tool, which keeps only schema, defaults and paging. Subject status is the `StavAbsolvovani` enum over the full 13-code `STAV_ABSOLVOVANI` domain, not the 3 codes one account happens to show. Per-subject `status` is a nested object (`type`/`code`/`label`/`superseded_by`) rather than four flat fields, and the "not passed, period concluded" bucket is named `failed` throughout (enum, summary counts, schema) rather than the more clinical `not_completed`.

  The weighted average was reverse-engineered from a printed FAV transcript and reproduces its 2,05 over 41 credits exactly, live: only numeric-scale subjects count, an unfinished one scores 4 (read from the scale's `nevyplnenoHodnotaDoPrumeru`, not hardcoded), and zápočet-only subjects are excluded even when failed. A subject later retaken and passed drops out of the earlier year so it is not counted twice — which is why the summary is always computed over the full history even when the list is filtered. `gpa_passed_only` ships alongside as the finished-work view, deliberately named so it cannot be mistaken for the official figure. A subject the catalogue endpoint does not list has no credit value, which would otherwise silently zero its contribution to `credits_earned`/`credits_enrolled` and drop it out of both GPAs with no sign anything was excluded — `summary.uncredited` counts these explicitly instead.

  Found once the service got its own direct tests (`tests/Unit/Services/Grades`, `tests/Feature/Services/Grades`), rather than only ever running as a byproduct of `GetZnamkyToolTest`.
